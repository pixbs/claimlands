    What to look for

    Cover should arrive in blobs, not confetti — one lone hex of wheat is not a farm, and the renderer merges neighbouring tiles of a kind into a single parcelled zone, which only shows when tiles come in groups. Flip to Scattered to see what the same numbers look like when they are not clumped.

    It should also be spread over the whole planet rather than gathered on one face of the icosahedron, and it must never touch the sea. Turn the globe right round and check both.
